Chat Folders

Organize your ChatGPT chats into folders, right in the sidebar. Search, color-code and find any conversation in seconds.

Add to Chrome — it's free

Features

  • Folders inside ChatGPT's sidebar: create, rename, color, collapse, reorder.
  • Add the open chat to a folder in one click, or add many at once.
  • Drag and drop chats between folders.
  • Instant search across folders and chat titles.
  • Light and dark mode.
  • Backup to a JSON file.

Free & Pro

Free: 3 folders with unlimited chats. Pro: unlimited folders and sync across your computers.
